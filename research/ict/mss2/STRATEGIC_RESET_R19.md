# Strategic Reset after R19

Date: 2026-08-17

This mandatory reset covers R17 trend pullback re-acceleration, R18 positioning
unwind reversal, and R19 positioning rebuild continuation. It also incorporates
the repository-wide LF V10B candidate audit.

## 1. What economic edge is actually proved?

None under the MSS2 standard. No independent live-tradable sleeve satisfies
causal replay, discovery/validation stability, 2× cost, winner resilience,
frequency, drawdown, and sealed-holdout requirements together.

## 2. What remains a market statistic?

- Completed-trend liquidity is a causal market-path coordinate system.
- Direct reversal, acceptance/cascade, and liquidity decay are descriptive path
  facts, not robust entries.
- Aligned trend pullbacks occur, but the frozen multi-timeframe reclaim sequence
  does not pay costs.
- OI release and rebuild transitions are frequent positioning state changes,
  but neither the reversal nor continuation branch has unfiltered economic edge.
- LF V10B shows that long-horizon trend/reclaim trades can generate very large
  historical convex winners, but its full-window optimization and winner
  concentration prevent an edge claim.

## 3. Strongest current candidate

There is no promotable candidate. The strongest research prior is LF V10B, with
saved 1× dollar PF 8.29 and positive annual realized results, but it is a
contaminated full-window composite rather than independent evidence. Within
MSS2 proper, no R17–R19 cell approaches promotion.

## 4. Candidate KPI status

The independently rerun V10B path has 105 trades (2.53/month), 62-day longest
flat, 45.2% positive months, 21.13% realized-capital MDD at base costs and
25.20% at 2× costs. Top-five removal survives, but top-ten removal reduces
return PF below one and compounded return below zero. Its historical window has
no untouched holdout, and the saved artifact does not exactly reproduce under
current code/data. MSS2 holdout outcomes remain unused for strategy selection.

secondary gaps are winner concentration, monthly consistency, and coverage.

perform one fixed-rule, visible-window falsification of the repository's only

with exact causal replay. Continuing price/OI sign-transition variants would be
research for its own sake. Auditing the sole large historical candidate is
closer to the money objective because it distinguishes real convexity from
optimization, leverage, compounding, and winner dependence. It must remain a
bounded falsification, not a new filter factory.
