# R04 Research Notes

R04 changes the question from “what is the best single TP?” to “what opportunity horizon does this causal liquidity event support?”

Frozen principles:

- keep short 0.3–1% and multi-day 3–5% opportunities in the same research universe;
- do not force a time stop;
- keep causal features physically separate from future labels;
- one rule gets one first qualifying stage per episode;
- 4H liquidity is a decision point to study, not automatically a full exit;
- partial-profit coverage is algebra only in R04, not a tuned position-management strategy;
- no new CVD/footprint/displacement threshold search in this version;
- if R04 reveals stable horizon transitions, R05 may test a causal opportunity-horizon classifier and/or partial+runner structural trailing.
