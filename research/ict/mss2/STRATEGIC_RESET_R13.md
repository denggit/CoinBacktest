## 1. What economic edge has actually been proved?

No live-tradable strategy sleeve has yet been proved. Completed-trend liquidity gives a causal and economically meaningful path framework, and some SSL reversal subsets show positive historical expectancy, but none survives all required checks: discovery/validation stability, 2x costs, top-winner removal, sufficient frequency, and portfolio-quality metrics.

- These are descriptive state variables, not proved entry rules.
