# Strategic Reset after R16

Date: 2026-08-16

This mandatory reset covers R14 liquidity acceptance, R15 fixed-R first passage, and R16 invalidation semantics. It follows the earlier reset after R13.

## 1. What economic edge is actually proved?

None. No independent live-tradable sleeve satisfies discovery/validation stability, 2x cost, top-winner resilience and frequency requirements.

## 2. What remains a market statistic?

- Completed-trend liquidity is a useful causal coordinate system.
- Direct reversal is a minority path with potentially large RR.
- Deeper-same-side-first is the dominant path class.
- Younger liquidity and expected response help classify paths.
- Persistent SSL acceptance and wider structural stops sometimes capture large continuation winners.

None of these facts alone produces a stable entry/exit strategy.

## 3. Strongest current candidate

There is no promotable candidate. R16 root-bar-extreme SSL continuation is the strongest coherent comparator at 2x PF 1.20 discovery and 1.07 at 3x discovery, but validation PF is only 0.36 and top-five-removed PF is 0.27/0.05. It is a rejected candidate, not a sleeve.

## 4. Candidate KPI status

The rejected R16 comparator has 4.29/6.50 entries per month and 40/23-day longest entry gaps, but only 41.7%/0% positive months and severe 2024 dependence. MDD, portfolio flat time, rolling 90d and account allocation are not computed because the edge gate fails before portfolio construction. Holdout is not opened.

## 5. Directions stopped or archived

- Universal completed-trend sweep reversal.
- Boolean reclaim/MSS/FVG reversal confirmation.
- BSL mirrored reversal and BSL acceptance continuation.
- Completed-trend SSL acceptance to distant same-side liquidity.
- 5/15-minute persistence threshold tuning.
- Fixed 0.5R–3R target rescue.
- Region-edge, root-bar-extreme and behavioral-reclaim stop rescue.
- Raw directional impulse/breakout continuation, already extensively rejected elsewhere in the repository.

## 6. Signs of filter or lifecycle rescue

Yes. R13 exposed attractive feature bins, R14 exposed sparse high-PF persistence cells, and R16 exposed a profitable 2024 stop variant. Each failed neighboring, validation or top-winner checks. These branches are now frozen to prevent another feature/stop/exit layer from rescuing historical winners.

## 7. Largest gap to the final goal

The largest gap is still a stable independent economic edge. Frequency, longest-flat coverage and portfolio smoothing are downstream. Improving sizing or leverage cannot compensate for negative validation expectancy.

## 8. Gap classification

- Edge strength/stability: critical.
- Regime dependence and winner concentration: critical.
- Long/Short balance: material.
- Frequency/coverage: material but premature.
- Execution cost relative to stop distance: material.
- Exit/position management: investigated and not the root cure.

## 9. Next-stage choice

Archive the completed-trend sweep strategy branch and search for a genuinely independent sleeve. The selected boundary is trend pullback continuation Long/Short, not breakout chasing:

- 1D/4H causal trend persistence and remaining runway;
- 1H/30m orderly pullback/compression;
- 15m/5m reclaim and re-acceleration;
- next-observable 1m execution;
- local structural stop plus volatility buffer and maximum stop-distance skip;
- one position, no add-ons, separate Long and Short evaluation.

R17 begins as a mechanism/path atlas. It may not inherit q70 scores, R13 bins, R14 rules, or a far completed-trend target. The existing mss2 holdout remains sealed relative to R17 selection. Because other repository projects have inspected overlapping 2025–2026 data, any eventual live approval will also require genuinely new forward data; this limitation must remain explicit.

## 10. Are we moving toward stable profit or researching for its own sake?

R14–R16 were useful because they tested and then closed the dominant inverse path without hiding cost, intrabar ordering or winner concentration. Continuing that branch would now be research for its own sake. Moving to a precommitted independent pullback mechanism is justified; it must be stopped quickly if simple path economics do not survive direction, year and cost checks.

## R17 precommitment

- First produce a causal trend/pullback/re-acceleration event atlas; no ML and no portfolio.
- Use a tiny set of structural definitions and neighboring sensitivity only.
- Breakout establishes state but never triggers entry.
- Long and Short must be reported separately.
- Maximum stop distance is declared before economic results.
- No holdout access, no q70 inheritance, no filter stacking.
- Do not proceed to execution overlays until a simple cross-year first-passage edge exists.
