- Occupied-signal add subsets were too small and concentrated.
- Static dual-risk slots lowered return by reserving risk.

- Score-tier risk sizing was not monotonic across years; equal risk was retained.
- Fixed waiting, no-chase waiting and pullback/reclaim entry all underperformed immediate entry.
